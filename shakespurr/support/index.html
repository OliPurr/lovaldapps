Shakespurr support

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Shakespurr is a translator for cats. It listens, works out what your cat most likely means from the sound it made, and delivers the meaning as a line from a Shakespearean stage. Treat every answer as an interpretation, offered with confidence and open to correction. Your recordings stay on your phone and disappear after the result unless you keep them in your history.

Something went wrong

Settings holds a support log with the app's last fifty events. It holds no audio, no names or other profile text, no file paths and no purchase tokens. Open Settings, Support log, tap Copy log, and paste it into your message.

I bought Pro and the banners are back

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. The store confirms your purchase and the banners go. Pro belongs to your store account, so it comes with you to a new phone, and Restore brings it back there too. Pro bought through Google Play stays with Google Play, and Pro bought through the App Store stays with the App Store.

Shakespurr heard no cat

When it hears no cat sound inside the six seconds, Shakespurr answers in its own voice and keeps nothing. Bring the phone within arm's length of your cat, choose a quiet moment, and let your cat speak inside the six seconds; once your cat has spoken, a second tap ends the listening and the answer comes sooner.

Your privacy

How the app handles your data is set out in the privacy policy. The answers to the questions most often asked are in the app, under Settings, FAQ.
