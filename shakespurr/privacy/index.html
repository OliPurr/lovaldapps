Shakespurr privacy policy Last updated: 20 September 2026 Shakespurr is a translator for cats. It listens, works out what your cat most likely means from the sound it made, and delivers the meaning as a line from a Shakespearean stage. Treat every answer as an interpretation, offered with confidence and open to correction. Your recordings stay on your phone and disappear after the result unless you keep them in your history. That last sentence is the short version of this page. The rest is the detail. What stays on your phone Everything the app makes is stored on your device and nowhere else. Your recordings. When you tap to translate, the app records a few seconds of sound. That audio is used on the phone to work out what your cat is doing, and it is never transmitted anywhere. It is deleted as soon as you leave the result, unless you have turned on Keep meows in history, in which case it stays on your phone until you delete the entry or the app. Your cats' profiles. Names, titles, photos and notes stay on the device. A photo you choose is copied into the app's own storage, resized, and stripped of its metadata, including any location the camera recorded, and the copy is deleted when you replace the photo or delete the profile. Your history and your feedback. Past results and the Fit? and Good line? answers are stored on the phone. Delete all history removes the results, any kept recordings and the feedback together. Your settings, and a small support log of what the app did, which holds no audio, no file paths and nothing you typed. You can read it or clear it in Settings. Your purchases, as a local record that you own them. None of this is sent to us. There are no accounts, and the app works without a network connection. Advertising The free version shows banner advertisements on the Home and History screens. They are requested as non-personalised ads, which is the setting that uses the least data. Advertising still involves data collection by Google's advertising software inside the app, and it does not happen without your say-so: Where the law requires your consent, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and under the state privacy laws of the United States, the app shows Google's consent form before any advertising software is started, and shows no advertisements at all until you have answered it. You can change your answer at any time in Settings, Privacy options. If the form cannot be shown, the app runs without advertisements. What the advertising software collects is described in Google's own documentation for the version shipped in this release, and is declared category by category in this app's Google Play Data safety entry. Shakespurr Pro removes the advertisements. Once it is active the app makes no further advertising requests. Purchases Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details. When you buy something, Google Play gives the app a receipt. The app checks that receipt with Google Play on your phone and unlocks what you paid for. Nothing about the purchase is sent to us, and we run no server for it. What we do not do We do not transmit your recordings. Ever. We do not have accounts, and we do not ask who you are. There is no analytics software in the app, and no crash-reporting software. Google Play and Apple report crashes to us in aggregate under their own terms, as they do for every app. We do not sell anything about you to anyone. Children Shakespurr is a general-audience app. It is not directed at children, and it is not designed for them. Your choices Delete everything the app holds by uninstalling it, or use Delete all history and Reset settings inside the app. Change your advertising consent in Settings, Privacy options. Stop keeping recordings by turning off Keep meows in history; existing ones are deleted when you delete their history entries.
